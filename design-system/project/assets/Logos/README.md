- `kuroobi.svg`: the wordmark "KUROOBI". Stroke and fill are `currentColor`, so it is drawn in the text colour of where it is placed. The app places it at the top of the left nav in the `text` colour, 100px wide. Shown through `<img>` it renders black, so screens embed the SVG directly.
- `icon.svg`: the app icon. Stones on a square in the board's green (#77914e, the same as `board`); where it is shown rounds or crops the corners itself. The macOS app icon files (`crates/gui/icons/`) are the exception: macOS draws them as given, so they carry rounded corners (radius 230 of 1024) with a transparent outside. Its colours are fixed and do not change with the theme.
